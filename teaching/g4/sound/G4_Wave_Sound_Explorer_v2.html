Interactive Wave Model 互动波形模型

Move the sliders and watch the wave change in real time. 拖动滑块，观察波形实时变化。
Higher amplitude → taller wave. 振幅越大，波看起来越高。
Longer wavelength → crests farther apart. 波长越长，波峰间距越大。
Try this 试一试：
  • Make the amplitude larger. What happens? 把振幅调大，波形发生了什么变化？
  • Make the wavelength shorter. Are the waves closer together? 把波长调短，波是不是更密了？
  • Can you make a tall wave with a short wavelength? 你能做出“高而密”的波吗？

Example 3 — Pitch 音调

Use this example to connect wavelength with low pitch and high pitch. 用这张图帮助学生理解波长与低音、高音之间的联系。
Teaching note 教学说明： This is a simplified classroom model: in the same medium, a longer wavelength usually means a lower pitch, and a shorter wavelength usually means a higher pitch. ｜ 这是一个课堂简化模型：在同一介质中，较长的波长通常对应较低的音调，较短的波长通常对应较高的音调。

Important note 重要说明：
This is a simplified classroom model. In real life, pitch 音调 is mainly linked to frequency 频率. In the same medium, a longer wavelength usually means a lower pitch. 这是一个简化的课堂模型。现实中音调主要与频率有关；在同一介质中，波长越长，音调通常越低。
Try and compare 比较一下：
  • Large amplitude + long wavelength → loud and low. 大振幅 + 长波长 → 更响、更低。
  • Small amplitude + short wavelength → soft and high. 小振幅 + 短波长 → 更轻、更高。
